# Flux
### An AI-Powered Academic Planner for Students

**2026 Congressional App Challenge Submission**

## About Flux

Students have a lot to keep track of: assignments, exams, extracurricular activities, and schedules that constantly change. I created Flux because I wanted a better way to organize everything without having to manually rebuild my schedule every day.

Flux is an AI-powered academic planning application designed to bring a student's academic and personal commitments into one place. Instead of relying on a traditional to-do list, Flux aims to help students understand not just *what* they need to do, but *when* they can realistically do it.

## The Idea

Flux is built around three goals:

- **Simplify planning:** Organize classes, assignments, tests, and extracurricular activities in one place.
- **Make schedules personal:** Allow students to build a planner around their actual school schedule.
- **Use AI meaningfully:** Help students prioritize tasks and adjust plans when their schedules change.

## Features and Development

Flux is currently under development, with an emphasis on creating an intuitive and accessible student experience.

### Core Planner
- Daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly planning views
- Class-period-based schedules
- Assignment and test detail screens
- Space for extracurricular activities and recurring commitments

### AI-Powered Planning
- AI assistant interface for academic planning
- Personalized scheduling and task-prioritization features under development
- Planned schedule adjustments based on upcoming deadlines

### Planned Integrations
- Google Classroom assignment synchronization
- Schedule extraction from uploaded school schedules
- Authentication and cloud-based data storage using Supabase
- Notifications and reminders

*Note: Some features are prototypes or planned functionality and are not yet fully implemented.*
